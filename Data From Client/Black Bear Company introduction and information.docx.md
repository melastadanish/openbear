Company Profile

Shenzhen Black Bear Smart Technology Co., Ltd. (abbreviation: Open Bear) was founded in Shenzhen, Guangdong in 2018\. It has always been committed to becom- ing a leading manufacturer in the global door control automation industry.  
The company focuses on the R\&D and manufacturing of high-end intelligent door control drive systems. Its product includes sliding door operators, swing door ope- rators, garage door operators, industrial door operators, high-speed door operators, servo barriers mechanism and controller and other series. It provides integrated solutions and core drive technical support for scenarios such as intelligent access control of vehicle parking and pedestrian.  
Open Bear focuses on servo motion control algorithms as its core technology. All product lines adopt **DC24V safety voltage servo permanent magnet synchronous motors**, delivering a smoother, more precise and safer door opening experience.  
Our products can replace traditional AC asynchronous motors, funda- mentally eliminating electric leakage risks and providing enhanced safety protection.  
Open Bear always adheres to the philosophy of **customer-priority**, taking market demands as the compass and customer satisfaction as the benchmark. Leveraging its robust R\&D capabilities, advanced intelligent manufacturing capacity, a rigorous quality management system and an agile service delivery, the company’s products are sold in many countries and regions worldwide.Together with its global partners, Open Bear is co-creating a smarter future for access control.
