## 9. FAQ

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

**H2:** What People Ask Before Buying This Side Mounted Swing Door Operator

**Q1: Can I control this from my phone?**  
Yes. Bluetooth and WiFi are both built into the unit — no add-on module required. Connect it to your home WiFi and open or close the gate from anywhere using the app. Bluetooth works within 15 metres when you arrive home.

**Q2: Which access methods does it support?**  
Five — push button, IC card reader, microwave sensor, the included RF remote controls, and a face recognition terminal. Every method connects to the same controller. You can run one or several simultaneously depending on your security setup.

**Q3: My gate is around 180 kg. Will this handle it?**  
Yes. The rated maximum is 200 kg for a single swing leaf up to 1.5 m wide. A 180 kg gate sits comfortably within that range. If your gate is heavier or the leaf is longer than 1.5 m, the Heavy-Duty Swing Gate Opener handles leaves up to 1,200 kg and 6 m.

**Q4: How loud is it during operation?**  
Running noise is rated at 60 dB or below — roughly the level of a normal conversation at close range. From inside your house with a door or window between you and the gate, you are unlikely to hear it at all.

**Q5: Is it safe if a child or pet gets near the gate while it is moving?**  
The motor runs on 24V DC — below the voltage threshold that causes electric shock. Add a photocell sensor and the gate stops and reverses immediately if the beam is broken mid-swing. Our team can advise on compatible sensors before you order.

**Q6: What happens during a power cut?**  
With a battery backup module fitted, the gate continues to open and close normally through a power cut — no manual release, no getting locked in or out. Ask our team which backup module is compatible when you enquire.

**Q7: How hard is it to install a side mounted swing door operator?**  
The kit ships complete — motor unit (6.4 kg total packaged weight), swing arm, crank connecting rod assembly, two fixing plates, two remote controls, and a full screw pack. Nothing needs to be sourced separately. At 355 mm long and 6.4 kg, the unit mounts to most standard gateposts without heavy bracket work. Most installers complete the full installation in a few hours. An English installation guide, wiring diagram, and parameter table are included in every box.

**Q8: How does Open Bear's side mounted swing door operator compare to FAAC or Nice?**  
Nice and FAAC both produce side-mounted swing operators in the light residential range — and both are sold through dealer networks, adding a reseller margin to the price. Open Bear's model handles gates up to 200 kg at 69.7°/s and uniquely includes Bluetooth, WiFi, and RF remote control as standard. Many comparable Nice and FAAC models charge separately for connectivity accessories. This is the only CE certified model in the Open Bear swing range — independently verified to European safety standards — and it ships factory-direct with no middleman in the chain.
