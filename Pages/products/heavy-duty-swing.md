## 9. FAQ

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

**H2:** What People Ask Before Buying a Heavy Duty Swing Gate Opener

**Q1: I have two gate leaves — do I need two motors?**  
Yes. Each motor drives one leaf. For a dual swing gate setup, you order two units and connect them to a dual-output controller — a single box that signals both motors to open and close together in sync. Our team can advise on the right controller configuration before you order.

**Q2: Which access methods does it support?**  
The opener is compatible with five access methods — push button, IC card reader, microwave sensor, the included RF remote controls, and a face recognition terminal. Every method connects to the same controller. You can run one or several simultaneously depending on your security setup.

**Q3: Do I need anything extra for phone control?**  
No. Bluetooth and WiFi are built directly into the unit. Open your gate from your phone and commission parameters remotely from day one — no extra module, no accessories, nothing to source separately.

**Q4: How loud is it when it opens?**  
The motor runs at 65 dB or below — similar to a normal conversation from a metre away. You will hear the gate moving. From inside a house or office with windows closed, you will not.

**Q5: What happens when the power goes out?**  
Press the orange manual release on the housing and the motor disengages — your gate swings freely by hand in seconds. For sites where you need automatic operation through a power cut, the controller supports a battery backup module.

**Q6: Does it work in extreme cold or heat?**  
The operating range runs from -35°C to 70°C. The same unit installs in Canada, Australia, and the Middle East without modification or special variants — the spec holds in every climate we ship to.

**Q7: Is it safe around children and pets?**  
The motor runs on 24V DC — below the voltage threshold that causes electric shock, even at 400W output. The obstacle detection system stops and reverses the gate immediately if it contacts anything during a cycle. For a gate this size, we recommend adding a photocell safety beam that halts the gate before contact occurs — our team can advise on compatible sensors before you order. The combination of DC24V voltage and active obstacle detection makes this operator the right choice for properties where children and animals are present.

**Q8: How hard is it to install a heavy duty swing gate opener?**  
The kit ships complete — motor unit at 19.1 kg, swing arm, anchor bolts, controller (packaged separately at 3 kg), two remote controls, and all mounting hardware in one box. Nothing needs to be sourced separately on installation day. Mechanical mounting involves concrete anchoring of the motor base — most installers complete this in a half day. Parameters are set once during commissioning and stored by the controller across power cycles. Full installation guide, wiring diagram, and parameter table are included in English.

**Q9: How does Open Bear's heavy duty swing gate opener compare to FAAC or Nice?**  
FAAC swing operators like the C720 and Nice's TT series are sold through distributor chains — which adds cost and extends lead times. Open Bear's heavy duty model delivers 650 Nm and handles gates up to 1,200 kg, putting it in the same bracket as FAAC's industrial swing range. The key difference: Bluetooth and WiFi are built in as standard on the Open Bear unit — comparable FAAC and Nice models at this weight class require separate accessory modules for phone connectivity. Factory-direct pricing means you deal with the manufacturer directly, with faster support and no reseller margin.
