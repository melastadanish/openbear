## 9. FAQ  

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

**H2:** What People Ask Before Buying a Sliding Gate Opener

**Q1: Which model do I need — 200W or 400W?**  
Weigh your gate leaf. If it is under 1,200 kg, the 200W handles it comfortably. If it is over 1,200 kg or you are not sure, choose the 400W — it covers gates up to 1,800 kg and uses the same controller and mounting system. When in doubt, our team can confirm the right model before you order.

**Q2: Which access methods does it support?**  
Five — push button, IC card reader, microwave sensor, the included RF remote controls, and a face recognition terminal. Every method connects to the same controller. You can run one or several simultaneously depending on your security setup.

**Q3: Can I control it from my phone?**  
Two RF remote controls ship with every kit, with a 15-metre range. For phone app control, a compatible WiFi accessory module connects the controller to your home network and lets you open or close the gate from anywhere via app. The module connects to the same controller that runs the gate — no rewiring required. Ask our team which WiFi module is compatible with this model before you order.

**Q4: How hard is it to install?**  
The kit arrives complete — motor, controller, two remotes, anchor bolts, end stops, and all mounting hardware in one box. Most homeowners use a local installer for the mechanical mounting and wiring. The installer sets the 10 controller parameters once during commissioning — the controller stores them and no re-entry is needed after that.

**Q5: How loud is the motor?**  
Both models run at 60 dB or below — roughly the level of a normal conversation at arm's length. From inside your house with windows closed, you will not hear it.

**Q6: What happens during a power cut?**  
The controller supports a battery backup module. With one fitted, the gate opens and closes normally through a power cut — no manual release, no getting locked out. Ask our team which backup module is compatible when you enquire.

**Q7: Is it safe if a child or pet gets near the gate while it is moving?**  
The motor runs on 24V DC — well below the voltage threshold that causes electric shock, so contact with the gate housing mid-cycle is not a shock risk. The obstacle detection system stops and reverses the gate the instant it contacts anything in its path — whether that is a child, a pet, or a vehicle. For additional protection, a photocell safety beam can be added to the installation to stop the gate before contact occurs. Our team can advise on compatible sensors before you order.

**Q8: How does Open Bear's sliding gate opener compare to FAAC or LiftMaster?**  
FAAC and LiftMaster sliding gate operators are well-established brands — but both sell through distributor and dealer networks, which adds reseller margins to the final price. Open Bear's sliding gate opener delivers the same DC24V servo motor and rack-and-pinion drive at a factory-direct price, with no reseller in the chain. The 400W model's 150 Nm torque and 1,800 kg capacity sits in the same performance bracket as FAAC's D600 range. Where Open Bear differs: both models ship complete with controller, remotes, and all hardware — no separate parts to source, no callbacks for missing accessories.
