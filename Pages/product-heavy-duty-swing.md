### [content-how-it-works·var-a]

eyebrow: HOW IT WORKS
h2: Four Components. One Gate That Opens Every Time.
subtitle: A heavy duty swing gate opener is a system — motor, controller, safety sensors, and power backup working together.

step-1-title: The Motor
step-1-body: The 400W servo permanent magnet synchronous motor runs on 24V DC. The unit's built-in power supply steps the mains voltage down to 24V DC before it reaches the motor — eliminating the electric leakage risk common to older AC gate systems and delivering 650 Nm of smooth, precise torque.

step-2-title: The Controller
step-2-body: The controller manages opening speed, closing speed, auto-close timing, and obstacle response. Set the parameters once during installation. The controller holds them across every cycle after that.

step-3-title: The Safety System
step-3-body: Every heavy duty swing gate opener in the Open Bear range supports photocell and safety edge integration. If anything breaks the beam or contacts the safety edge, the gate stops and reverses immediately — protecting children, pets, and vehicles without manual intervention.

step-4-title: Power Backup and Manual Release
step-4-body: When the grid goes down, the orange manual release lets you disengage the motor by hand and swing the gate open in seconds. For properties where power cuts are frequent, the controller also supports a battery backup module for fully automatic operation through an outage.

### [content-faq·var-a]

```
eyebrow: COMMON QUESTIONS
h2: What People Ask Before Buying a Heavy Duty Swing Gate Opener

q1: I have two gate leaves — do I need two motors?
a1: Yes. Each motor drives one gate leaf. For a dual swing gate opener setup, you order two units and connect them to a dual-output controller — a single box that signals both motors to open and close together. Our team can advise on the right controller configuration before you order.

q2: How loud is it when it opens?
a2: The motor runs at 65 dB or below — similar to a normal conversation from a metre away. You will hear the gate moving. From inside a house or office with windows closed, you will not.

q3: Can I control it from my phone?
a3: Two RF remote controls are included with the kit. For phone app control, a compatible WiFi accessory module connects the controller to your network and lets you trigger the gate from an app. Ask our team for the right accessory before you order.

q4: What happens when the power goes out?
a4: Press the orange manual release button on the housing and the motor disengages — your gate swings freely by hand in seconds. For sites where you need automatic operation through a power cut, add a battery backup module to the controller.
```
