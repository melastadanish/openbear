**Tile 1 — Is a barrier gate right for your site?**

If your entrance opens hundreds of times a day, a standard gate operator will not last. Car parks, logistics yards, toll lanes, petrol stations — these sites need a motor rated for volume. At 10 million cycles, this barrier handles roughly 1,000 full trips per day for 27 years.

If the application is a residential driveway or a commercial entrance that opens fewer than 50 times a day, a sliding or swing operator is the correct specification. Barrier gates are built for volume — specifying one on a low-cycle site is over-engineering and over-budget.

View the full gate operators range →

---

**Tile 2 — What arm length do you need?**

The barrier arm spans the lane. Maximum arm length on this model is 6 metres — correct for a standard single-lane vehicle entrance. For wider lanes or dual-barrier installations, confirm the lane width before ordering.

Carbon fibre arms are standard on this model. They are lighter than aluminium, which reduces motor stress on each trip and extends drive component life at high cycle rates.
