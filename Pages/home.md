### [content-how-it-works·var-a]

eyebrow: HOW IT WORKS
h2: Four Components. One Gate That Opens Every Time.
subtitle: An automatic gate opener is a system — motor, controller, safety sensors, and power backup working together.

step-1-title: The Motor
step-1-body: The DC24V servo motor is what moves your gate. It adjusts its output to match the weight of the door — sliding gates up to 1,800 kg, swing gates delivering up to 650 Nm of torque. This is the component that determines what your gate can handle.

step-2-title: The Controller
step-2-body: The controller manages opening speed, closing speed, auto-close timing, and obstacle response. The sliding operator gives you 10 settings to control — opening speed, closing speed, auto-close delay, obstacle response, and more. Set them once. The system holds them.

step-3-title: The Safety System
step-3-body: Obstacle detection stops the gate the instant it contacts anything in its path. Every automatic gate opener in the Open Bear range supports photocell and safety edge integration — protecting children, pets, and vehicles without manual intervention.

step-4-title: Power Backup
step-4-body: When the grid goes down, the gate keeps working. Battery backup is standard across the Open Bear range — your gate opens and closes normally through a power cut, no manual override needed.
