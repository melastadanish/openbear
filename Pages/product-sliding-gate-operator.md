**Eyebrow:** INSTALLATION

**H2:**
Three Steps to a Working Installation.

Correct tool reach is essential for silent operation. Mount base plates and affix gear racks with absolute precision.

**Step 01 — Foundation Casting**
Pour a stable concrete pad for the motor base plate. A solid foundation prevents vibration and eliminates noise transfer to the gate structure. It also protects the drive components from ground movement over years of operation.

**Step 02 — Gear Rack Alignment**
Weld or screw the steel gear rack along the full gate travel path. Rack alignment directly determines gate speed consistency and drive gear wear — misalignment by even a few millimetres increases component stress at every cycle.

**Step 03 — Safety System Configuration**
Wire and calibrate the photocells, safety edge, and obstacle response parameter (P-5 on the controller). A correctly configured safety system stops and reverses the gate on contact or beam-break — protecting people, vehicles, and the gate itself.

**Eyebrow:** THE TECHNOLOGY

**H2:**
Why DC24V Servo Motor Matters for Your Installation.

Most sliding gate motors run on AC. That means live voltage in the housing, on the rack, on the gate itself. On a wet morning — or when a child touches the wrong part — that is a liability no installer wants to explain.

Both Open Bear sliding gate operators run on 24V DC — below the threshold that causes electric shock. The motor voltage stays at a safe level regardless of what the mains supply does. No electric leakage risk. No insulation failures transferring to the gate structure.

Precise speed control at every point in the travel cycle is something AC motors cannot deliver. Soft-start from rest, steady full-speed transit, controlled deceleration before the end stop. No jolt. No slamming. The gate behaves exactly the way you programme it across all 10 controller parameters.

**H2:**
Common Questions About the Sliding Gate Operator

---

**Q1: What is the maximum gate weight the Open Bear sliding gate operator can handle?**

The Open Bear sliding gate operator is available in two models. The 200W model handles gates up to 1,200 kg. The 400W model handles gates up to 1,800 kg. Both figures are the rated maximum — not a peak or burst capacity. For gates near the upper limit of either model, the 400W is the safer specification. Running a motor consistently at or above its rated load will shorten drive component life and increase service frequency.

---

**Q2: Does this sliding gate operator work in extreme cold or heat?**

Both the 200W and 400W models operate from -35°C to 70°C. This covers the full range of installation environments across North America, northern Europe, Australia, and Southeast Asia. No cold-weather kit is required for Canadian or Russian winters. No high-temperature derating applies in Australian or Middle Eastern summers. The operating humidity tolerance is up to 95% non-condensing, which covers coastal and tropical installations.

---

**Q3: What does the kit include — do I need to source additional parts?**

Each sliding gate operator ships as a complete kit. The package includes the motor unit, the Linear Opener Controller (pre-configured, 10 programmable parameters), two remote controls, mounting hardware, anchor bolts, and the end stop. No additional components are required to commission the operator. English installation documentation and the controller parameter guide are included in every shipment.

---

**Q4: How loud is the sliding gate operator during operation?**

Both models run at ≤60 dB during normal operation. 60 dB is approximately the volume of a normal conversation at one metre. The rack-and-pinion drive with servo motor control eliminates the mechanical noise associated with chain-driven or AC motor alternatives. This makes both models suitable for residential installations adjacent to living areas, as well as commercial and industrial sites where noise is a compliance consideration.

---

**Q5: What is the 10-parameter controller and what can it adjust?**

The Linear Opener Controller included with both models has 10 independently adjustable parameters (P-1 through P-10). These cover: auto-close time, delay-close behaviour, open deceleration position, close deceleration position, obstacle response sensitivity, minimum speed, maximum speed, fall direction, and orientation speed. All parameters are set on-site using the controller panel — no software or app required. This level of programmability allows precise tuning for gates of different weights, travel distances, and traffic volumes.

---

**Q6: What type of drive system does this operator use?**

The Open Bear sliding gate operator uses a rack-and-pinion drive system. The motor output connects to a pinion gear that runs along a steel rack mounted on the gate. This is the most reliable drive method for heavy sliding gates — no stretch, no slip, and no gradual tension loss over time as seen in chain-driven systems. The rack-and-pinion format also allows easy manual release if power fails.

---

**Q7: Is this a factory-direct product — can I order directly from the manufacturer?**

Yes. Open Bear is the manufacturer. All orders ship factory-direct from Shenzhen — no distributor, no intermediary, no margin stacked on top. Annual production capacity is 50,000 units per year. OEM and ODM orders are available for distributors and agents who require branded product or customised specification. Contact the team via the form below to discuss pricing and minimum order quantities.

---

**Q8: Is the sliding gate operator compatible with safety sensors and access control systems?**

Yes. The controller board supports connection to photocells (safety beams), safety edges, and external access control inputs including keypads, intercoms, and card readers. The obstacle response parameter (P-5 on the controller) sets the sensitivity of the auto-reverse function — the gate stops and reverses if resistance is detected mid-travel. Safety sensor integration is standard on all professional installations and is supported across both models.
