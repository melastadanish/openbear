Hospital and healthcare environments place a specific set of demands on automatic door operators. Noise is the first: patient wards, ICUs, and recovery areas require doors that open and close without mechanical noise disturbing patients or staff. The side-mounted swing door operator runs at ≤60 dB — quieter than a normal conversation.

Safety is the second demand. On a healthcare site, any exposed electrical risk is unacceptable. The DC24V motor voltage in every Open Bear unit runs below the electric shock threshold — no leakage risk for patients, visitors, or maintenance staff.

Traffic is the third. Hospital main entrances, A&E departments, and ward corridors cycle doors hundreds of times per day. The side-mounted model handles up to 200 kg and operates at 69.7°/s — the fastest angular speed in the range — so doors open ahead of approaching traffic, not after it arrives.

**H2: What to Confirm Before Buying a Swing Door Operator for Hospitals**

- **Door weight and leaf length.** The side-mounted model handles up to 200 kg and leaves up to 1.5 m. Heavier or longer hospital entrance doors need the heavy-duty model (up to 1,200 kg, 6 m).
- **Activation method.** Hospital doors require hands-free activation — push plate, motion sensor, or access control integration. Confirm the controller's input compatibility before ordering.
- **Noise compliance.** Verify the ≤60 dB spec against your facility's acoustic requirements. This figure is measured at rated load and speed.
- **Maintenance access.** The side-mounted operator mounts on the door frame exterior. Service access does not require door removal.
