A warehouse gate runs dozens to hundreds of cycles per day. Forklifts, trucks, and yard vehicles cycle it around the clock. It sits exposed to weather — sun, rain, cold — with no maintenance between service visits. Most gate operators aren't built for that duty cycle. The sliding gate operator for warehouses is.

The 400W model handles gates up to 1,800 kg. That covers steel-framed sliding gates at large-span warehouse entrances, cold storage facilities, and logistics centre loading docks. Operating temperature range is -35°C to 70°C — the same unit runs in a Canadian cold storage yard and an Australian freight depot without modification.

Open Bear ships [gate operators](/gate-operators) as a complete kit — motor, controller, remotes, mounting hardware, and anchor bolts. The [sliding gate operator](/sliding-gate-operator) uses a rack-and-pinion drive system. No belt, no chain — fewer wear components, longer service life between maintenance.

**H2: What to Confirm Before Buying a Sliding Gate Operator for Warehouses**

- **Gate weight.** Measure the full gate panel including frame, infill, and hardware. 1,800 kg is the rated maximum for the 400W model. Gates at or above 1,200 kg should use the 400W, not the 200W.
- **Track and rack condition.** The operator drives the rack mounted on the gate. Worn, misaligned, or poorly fitted rack increases motor load — check rack pitch and alignment before installation.
- **Power supply.** The motor runs on DC24V from the supplied controller. The controller requires a 220VAC mains supply at the motor position. Confirm cable run distance before installation.
- **Duty cycle.** At warehouse cycling rates (50+ cycles/day), confirm the controller's auto-close and obstacle response settings are configured before handing the site over.
