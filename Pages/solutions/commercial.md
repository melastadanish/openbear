**Eyebrow:**
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:**
Common Questions About Commercial Gate Operators.

**Q1: What types of commercial gate operators does Open Bear supply?**
Open Bear supplies three commercial gate operators: the high-speed barrier gate, the heavy-duty swing gate operator, and the 400W sliding gate operator. The barrier gate is suited to parking lots, hospitals, and logistics entries — rated to 10,000,000 life cycles with a trip time as fast as 0.7 seconds. The heavy-duty swing operator handles entrance gates up to 6 metres and 1,200 kg. The sliding operator handles perimeter sliding gates up to 1,800 kg with a 10-parameter programmable controller.

**Q2: How many daily cycles can a commercial barrier gate handle?**
The Open Bear barrier gate is rated to 10,000,000 life cycles with a mean cycles between failures of 2,000,000. A car park processing 800 cycles per day reaches 2,000,000 cycles in approximately 6.8 years — well within the mean cycles between failures rating. At 500 cycles per day, the gate runs for over 54 years before reaching its full life cycle limit.

**Q3: What happens to a commercial gate if the power goes out?**
Every commercial model has a manual release built in. In a power outage, a staff member disengages the motor by hand and pushes the gate to the required position — open or closed. No tools are needed. The gate can be secured manually until power is restored, and no technician call is required.

**Q4: Is DC24V safe voltage relevant for commercial sites?**
Yes — particularly for hospitals, schools, and any site where staff or members of the public are regularly near the gate structure. A permanent magnet synchronous motor running at 24V DC cannot produce leakage current onto a metal gate frame that poses a shock risk on contact. This is why DC24V specification is increasingly required or preferred in regulated commercial environments.

**Q5: What temperature range do commercial operators handle?**
All three commercial models operate from -35°C to 70°C. This covers Open Bear's full primary market range — North American winters, Middle Eastern summers, and Southeast Asian humidity up to 95% non-condensing. No weatherproofing enclosures or cold-start accessories are required for any outdoor commercial installation in these ranges.

**Q6: How fast does the barrier gate open and close?**
Trip time on the barrier gate is adjustable from 0.7 to 6 seconds per cycle. The 0.7-second speed is achieved with a carbon fibre arm — the lightest arm option. An aluminium arm achieves a minimum trip time of 0.9 seconds. For a high-volume entry point, the faster setting processes more vehicles per minute without creating a queue.

**Q7: Is it hard to install a commercial gate operator?**
Every Open Bear commercial operator ships with a full installation manual, wiring diagram, and parameter table in English. The barrier gate mounts to a standard base plate included in the kit. The heavy-duty swing operator mounts to the gate post with the included bracket hardware. Most commercial installations are completed by a qualified gate or electrical technician in a single day — the documentation is clear enough that no factory support call is typically needed during commissioning.
