### SECTION 8: `section-faq`


**Eyebrow:**
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:**
Common Questions About Residential Gate Operators.

**Q1: What gate weights do your residential operators handle?**
The residential range covers gates from 200 kg up to 1,200 kg depending on the model. The side-mounted and top-mounted swing operators handle up to 200 kg. The floor swing model handles up to 250 kg. The sliding gate operator handles up to 1,200 kg — the highest capacity in the residential range.

**Q2: What happens if the power goes out?**
Every motor has a manual release built in. When the power goes out, you disengage the motor manually and push the gate open by hand. No tools required. No locksmith. You are never trapped in or out.

**Q3: How loud is the motor during operation?**
Every residential model runs at or below 60 dB during operation — the equivalent of a quiet conversation. You will hear the gate move, but not the motor working.

**Q4: Can I control the gate from my phone?**
The side-mounted swing operator has Bluetooth and WiFi built in — open and close from your smartphone without any additional hardware. All other models ship with encrypted remote controls included.

**Q5: Does everything I need come in the box?**
Yes. Every Open Bear operator ships as a complete kit — motor, controller, remote controls, mounting brackets, anchor bolts, and hardware. Nothing needs to be sourced separately before installation.

**Q6: What climate conditions can these motors handle?**
Residential swing models are rated from -25°C to 70°C. The sliding gate operator is rated from -35°C to 70°C. No additional weatherproofing is required for outdoor residential installation in any of these temperature ranges.

**Q7: Is it hard to install an automatic driveway gate operator?**
Installation difficulty depends on the model and your gate type. Every Open Bear operator ships with a full installation manual, wiring diagram, and parameter table in the box — in plain English. Most residential swing operators are installed by a single electrician or gate technician in a half-day. The floor-concealed model requires more site preparation — a recess in the ground substrate — and is typically a full-day installation.

**Q8: How does Open Bear compare to MightyMule for residential driveway gates?**
MightyMule targets light residential gates up to around 300 kg with consumer-grade AC motors. Open Bear residential models handle gates up to 1,200 kg on the sliding operator, and run on DC24V servo motors — permanent magnet synchronous motors that eliminate electric leakage risk entirely. Unlike AC motor designs, the DC24V servo motor adjusts torque in real time as the gate moves, which means smoother operation at any gate weight and no hard slamming at open or close. Open Bear ships factory-direct from Shenzhen — no reseller markup in the chain.
