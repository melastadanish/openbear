**H2:**
Safety Features Built for Industrial Environments.

**Tile 1 — DC24V Safe Voltage**
Industrial gate structures are metal. Every Open Bear motor runs on 24V DC — a permanent magnet synchronous motor at safe-touch voltage — which means electric leakage onto the gate frame is not possible. No shock risk for workers in contact with the gate.

**Tile 2 — F-Class Thermal Protection**
F-class winding insulation is rated to 155°C — the same thermal protection class used in industrial motor applications worldwide. The motor will shut down safely before it reaches a damaging temperature, never before it reaches its rated operating limit.

**Tile 3 — Obstacle Detection**
The motor stops and reverses on resistance above the threshold. Set the sensitivity via the controller — firm enough to stop a vehicle intrusion, sensitive enough to reverse on unexpected contact with a person.

**Tile 4 — Temperature Rise Cap**
Under full continuous load, motor temperature rises no more than 15°C above ambient — across both industrial models. That is sustained performance at rated capacity, not a peak figure achieved briefly then lost.

### SECTION 8: `section-faq`


**Eyebrow:**
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**H2:**
Common Questions About Industrial Gate Operators.

**Q1: What gate weights can an industrial gate operator handle?**
Open Bear industrial gate operators handle gates from 1,200 kg up to 1,800 kg depending on the model. The heavy-duty swing operator handles swing gates up to 1,200 kg and 6 metres in leaf length. The 400W sliding operator handles sliding gates up to 1,800 kg — the highest capacity in the Open Bear range. Both run on 400W DC24V servo motors rated for continuous industrial operation.

**Q2: What happens to the gate if the power goes out on-site?**
Every industrial model has a manual release built in. When power is lost, a site worker disengages the motor manually and pushes the gate open by hand. No tools are required, and no technician needs to be called. The gate can be secured in the open or closed position until power is restored.

**Q3: Is a DC24V motor safe on a metal industrial gate structure?**
Yes. DC24V is below the threshold where electric current causes injury on contact. A permanent magnet synchronous motor at 24V DC cannot produce leakage current onto a metal gate frame that poses a shock risk to workers. This is a key reason industrial buyers in regulated markets specify DC24V motors over traditional AC alternatives.

**Q4: What temperature range do the industrial models operate in?**
Both industrial models — the 400W sliding operator and the heavy-duty swing operator — are rated from -35°C to 70°C. This covers the full climate range of Open Bear's primary markets, including Canadian winters, Middle Eastern summers, and Southeast Asian humidity up to 95% non-condensing. No additional weatherproofing or cold-start accessories are required.

**Q5: How loud is the motor during operation at an industrial site?**
Both industrial models run at or below 60 dB during operation. For reference, 60 dB is roughly equivalent to a normal conversation at close range. At an active warehouse or factory site, the motor will not be audible above ambient noise.

**Q6: Can I control the industrial gate operator remotely?**
Every model ships with remote controls included. The sliding operator's 10-parameter controller can be programmed for auto-close, delayed close, and access logging. For integration with site access control systems, the controller accepts external trigger inputs — wiring details are in the included documentation.

**Q7: Is it hard to install an industrial gate operator on-site?**
Installation complexity depends on gate type and site conditions. Every Open Bear industrial operator ships with a full installation manual, wiring diagram, and parameter table — in English. The sliding gate operator mounts to a standard rack-and-pinion track system. The heavy-duty swing operator mounts to the gate post with the included bracket hardware. Most installations are completed by a qualified electrical or gate technician in a single day.
