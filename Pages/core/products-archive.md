### [content-faq·var-a]

```
eyebrow: COMMON QUESTIONS
h2: What People Ask Before Choosing a Gate Operator

q1: Which gate operator is right for my property?
a1: It comes down to your gate type and weight. For a gate that slides along a track, choose the sliding operator — it handles up to 1,800 kg. For a gate that swings open, the right model depends on the leaf length and weight: the side-mounted operator suits gates up to 200 kg and 1.5 m, the heavy-duty swing handles up to 1,200 kg and 6 m. For car parks or high-traffic access points, the barrier gate is the right tool — it completes a full trip in 0.7 seconds and is rated for 10,000,000 cycles.

q2: Are all models safe to use around children and pets?
a2: Every model in the range runs on 24V DC — well below the voltage threshold where electric shock becomes a risk. All controllers support photocell and safety edge integration, so the gate stops and reverses immediately if anything breaks the beam mid-cycle.

q3: Do the motors work in cold or hot climates?
a3: The sliding and barrier gate operators run from -35°C to 70°C — covering Canadian winters and Middle Eastern summers without modification. The swing operators run from -25°C to 70°C, which covers most residential climates worldwide.

q4: Does everything ship in one box?
a4: Yes. Every model ships as a complete kit — motor unit, controller, two remote controls, and all mounting hardware arrive together. Nothing needs to be sourced separately before installation day.

q5: Can I control any of these from my phone?
a5: The side-mounted swing operator has Bluetooth and WiFi built in — no extra module needed. For other models, a compatible WiFi accessory connects the controller to your home network. Our team can advise on the right accessory for your chosen model before you order.

q6: Do these work during a power cut?
a6: Every controller in the range supports battery backup connection. With a backup module fitted, the gate continues to open and close normally through a power cut. Ask our team which battery module is compatible with your chosen model when you enquire.
```
