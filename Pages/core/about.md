## Section 2 — The Problem with Most Gate Openers

**Eyebrow:** WHY IT MATTERS WHERE YOU SOURCE

**H2:** Most Gate Opener Suppliers Have a Problem. Three of Them.

**Body:**

Start with the motor. Most gate openers on the market run on AC asynchronous motors. AC motors carry a live electric leakage risk — your customers' gate housings are live to the touch. Open Bear uses DC24V servo permanent magnet synchronous motors across the full range. The 24V voltage sits well below the electric shock threshold. No leakage risk. No live motor housing.

Then there is the supply chain. Most suppliers calling themselves manufacturers are not. They buy from a factory, mark up the price, and pass the order along — and when quality fails, there is no one with actual production authority to fix it. Open Bear is the factory. The same 25-person team that runs R&D also runs production and quality control, in one building, on one line.

The kit is the third issue. Most shipments arrive missing something. Installers spend time sourcing parts that should have been in the box — manuals in a language no one reads, wiring diagrams that don't match the unit, controllers without mounting hardware. Every Open Bear order ships complete: motor, controller, remotes, all hardware, and English documentation in the same box.

Open Bear was built to solve all three.

## Section 5 — Certifications

**Eyebrow:** CERTIFICATIONS

**H2:** Verified Before It Ships. Certified Where It Counts.

**Body:**
Every Open Bear unit passes motor signal verification on an oscilloscope before it leaves the production line. That is the per-unit standard — not batch sampling, not end-of-run spot checks. The side-mounted swing door operator carries CE certification. That is the only Open Bear product with that designation — stated here precisely because the certification matters and because accuracy builds more trust than a blanket claim.

**Certification table:**
