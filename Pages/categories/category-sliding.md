**Tile 1 — Is sliding right for your gate?**

Sliding gates travel parallel to the fence — they need clear run space on one side of the opening, not clearance in front of it. That makes them the right specification wherever space in front of the gate is limited: sloped driveways, tight urban yards, or commercial vehicle entrances where swing clearance is not possible.

If the driveway is level and there is adequate clearance in front of the gate, a swing operator may be the better choice. If there is any doubt about swing clearance, specify sliding.

Compare sliding gate operator vs swing gate operator →

---

**Tile 2 — 200W or 400W?**

Gate weight is the specification that matters most. The 200W model handles up to 1,200 kg — correct for most residential and light commercial sliding gates. The 400W handles up to 1,800 kg — the specification for industrial-weight gates, heavy steel, or any gate where the 200W is at or near its rated capacity.

Weigh the gate before specifying. If the gate weight is within 10–15% of the rated maximum, size up.
