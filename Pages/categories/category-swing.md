**Intro line:**

---

**Tile 1 — How heavy is your gate leaf?**

Weight drives the specification. The heavy-duty model handles up to 1,200 kg and 6 metres — it is the only correct choice for large metal entrance gates. For lighter swing gates up to 200–250 kg, the side-mounted, top-mounted, or floor models are the right fit.

Weigh the leaf and measure the width before specifying. Running a motor at its rated limit shortens its life significantly.

View heavy duty swing gate operator →

---

**Tile 2 — What type of installation are you working with?**

Installation type determines which swing model is viable. Side-mounted motors attach to the gate post and arm. Top-mounted motors require a flat surface above the door frame. Floor-concealed models need a minimum floor depth — typically 150–200 mm depending on the substrate.

No suitable surface or insufficient floor depth? Specify an arm-mounted model.

View floor swing door operator →

---

**Tile 3 — Do you need concealed hardware?**

For architectural and high-end residential installations, visible gate hardware is a design problem. The floor swing operator is the answer — the entire mechanism sits below floor level. Once commissioned, there is nothing above ground to see.

If concealed installation is not the priority and a visible motor is acceptable, the side-mounted or top-mounted models offer simpler installation and lower site preparation cost.

View side-mounted swing door operator →
