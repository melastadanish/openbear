## Gate / Door Types

**Sliding:** straight, cantilever (no ground track), telescopic, tracked, heavy/large-span
**Swing:** single-leaf, double-leaf (bi-parting), in-swing, out-swing, glass swing door
**Barrier:** straight boom, folding/articulated, fence boom, telescopic boom

**Attributes (page modifiers):** material (aluminium/steel/stainless) · weight class (up to 300/600/1000kg+) · span (standard/wide) · power (110V/220V/24V DC/solar)
