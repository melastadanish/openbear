## Copywriting Style — NICE Method (Primary Reference)

This is the core writing discipline for all Open Bear copy. Derived from analysis of niceforyou.com.

### The Proof Formula
Every claim follows this exact structure — no exceptions:

```
OUTCOME HEADLINE (5–8 words)
One sentence connecting the experience to the number.
Spec chip — the raw number alone.
```

**Example applied to Open Bear:**
> Silent Every Time.
> Every motor in the range runs at or below 60 dB — quieter than a normal conversation.
> *≤60 dB · All models*

The outcome earns attention. The sentence earns belief. The spec chip closes it. Nothing more.

### Headline Rules
- State what the buyer **experiences or gains** — never what the product **has**
- 5–8 words maximum for section headlines
- 3–4 words for card/feature labels (NICE style: "Powerful. Installer Friendly. Solar Optimized.")
- No adjectives without a number behind them — "powerful" never appears alone

### Sentence Rules
- One idea per sentence
- Maximum 20 words per sentence
- Active voice only — no passive constructions
- "You" appears more than "we" in every section
- Short sentences (5–8 words) mixed with medium (12–18 words) — never three long sentences in a row

### Proof Rules
- Every benefit needs one spec number behind it — not a paragraph, one number
- Spec numbers appear as chips or short labels — not buried in body copy
- Numbers are exact: "≤60 dB" not "very quiet" — "650 Nm" not "powerful torque"
- Proof comes after the outcome, never before

### Banned Words & Phrases
- "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "world-class", "next-generation"
- "seamlessly", "robust", "leverage", "elevate", "empower", "innovative"
- "revolutionize", "game-changing", "industry-leading", "tailor-made"
- "In today's world", "In the modern era"
- "Whether you're looking for...", "Look no further"
- "Our team of experts", "Dedicated team", "Passionate professionals"
- "Peace of mind" — overused, replace with specific outcome
- "Reliable" alone — always follow with a spec: "Reliable. Rated for 10 million cycles."
- "High quality" — meaningless without a number
- Any CTA that says "Learn More" or "Click Here" — always specific

### CTA Rules (NICE Method)
Every CTA tells the user exactly what they get when they click:
- ✅ "Download the sliding operator spec sheet"
- ✅ "Compare all six models"
- ✅ "Find the right opener for your gate"
- ❌ "Learn more"
- ❌ "Click here"
- ❌ "Contact us" (acceptable only in contact sections)

### Product Card Structure (NICE Method)
```
One-line positioning statement — what makes this model the right choice
Key outcome sentence — what it does for the user
Spec chips — 3 max, exact numbers only
CTA — specific action
```

### Section Structure (NICE Method)
```
Eyebrow — 2–3 word category label, uppercase
H2 — outcome headline, 5–10 words
Subtext — one sentence of context, 15–20 words max
Content — cards / copy / table
```

## Copywriting Frameworks

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
Use for: Technology section, FAQ answers, objection handling
- Problem: Manual gates are a security gap
- Agitate: AC motors add a shock risk your family doesn't know about
- Solution: 24V DC eliminates that risk at the source

### FAB (Feature → Advantage → Benefit)
Use for: Why-section cards, product feature highlights
- Feature: DC24V servo motor
- Advantage: Runs below the electric shock threshold
- Benefit: Safe to touch — for installers and homeowners

### AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
Use for: Hero section, product page intros
- Attention: Outcome headline
- Interest: One-sentence proof
- Desire: Spec chips + use case image
- Action: Specific CTA

### StoryBrand
Use for: Overall page narrative
- Customer is the hero (homeowner, installer, property manager)
- Gate opener is the tool
- Open Bear is the guide — the manufacturer who knows the product

## SEO Standards

### Keyword Placement (Per Page)
- H1: exact-match primary keyword — no creative rewording
- First sentence of opening paragraph: primary keyword
- One H2 mid-page: primary keyword or close variation
- One body paragraph: primary keyword (not same section as H2)
- One image alt text: keyword in context
- CTA section body copy: primary keyword
- Meta description: primary keyword in first 10 words

### Meta Tag Format
- Title: `[Primary Keyword] | Open Bear` — max 60 characters
- Description: Benefit-led sentence, primary keyword in first 10 words — max 155 characters

### Internal Linking Rules
- Anchor text must be the exact target keyword of the destination page
- Never use "click here", "learn more", "this page" as anchor text
- Minimum 2 internal links per page
- Follow SILO structure — only link within correct tier paths
