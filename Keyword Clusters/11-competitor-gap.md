## Strategic Takeaway
1. **MightyMule** dominates residential driveway keywords — Open Bear should NOT fight here directly
2. **LiftMaster** dominates branded terms — Open Bear should target non-branded alternatives
3. **Both competitors are weak on B2B manufacturer keywords** — this is Open Bear's best opportunity
4. **KD scores are remarkably low** (0–8) across core terms — significant SEO opportunity
5. **Manufacturer + wholesale + supplier keywords** have near-zero competition — go after these first
