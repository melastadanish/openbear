## BLOCK: `[content-buyer-advice·var-a]` — 5 Mistakes When Choosing a Gate Opener Manufacturer

*Use on: archive page, about page. Audience: distributors and resellers evaluating Open Bear as a supply source.*

**Eyebrow:** SUPPLIER SELECTION

**H2:** 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing a Gate Opener Manufacturer

**Subtitle:** Most sourcing problems don't show up at the factory — they show up six months later, in the field. Here's what to check before you commit to a supply partner.

**Mistake 1 — Choosing a trading company over a factory**
Trading companies add margin and lose control over production quality. Open Bear is the manufacturer — 50,000 units a year come off our own production line in Shenzhen. No middlemen between you and the source.

**Mistake 2 — Not verifying load ratings independently**
A spec sheet is not a test report. Ask for oscilloscope waveform verification data. Every Open Bear unit is tested individually on a motor signal oscilloscope before it leaves the factory — not batch sampled.

**Mistake 3 — Ignoring temperature range for your target market**
A product rated to -10°C will fail in Canadian winters. Open Bear industrial and heavy-duty models are rated -35°C to 70°C — covering North America, Northern Europe, and the Middle East without special variants.

**Mistake 4 — Skipping English documentation**
Your installers and end customers need manuals they can read. All Open Bear products ship with full English documentation — wiring diagrams, parameter tables, troubleshooting guides, and installation manuals.

**Mistake 5 — No OEM or ODM path**
If your business grows, you need a manufacturer that can grow with you. Open Bear supports OEM and ODM orders — custom branding, modified specs, and private label supply — without minimum order premiums on standard models.

Before any unit leaves the factory, it is tested on an oscilloscope — waveform verification of the motor signal under load. Not batch testing. Every unit.
