## BLOCK: `[content-how-it-works·var-a]` — How a Gate Operator System Works

**Eyebrow:** TECHNICAL INSIGHT

**H2:** How a Gate Operator System Works

**Subtitle:** Understanding the fundamentals helps you specify the right system — and avoid costly mistakes on site.

**Step 1 — The Motor Drive**
Open Bear systems use DC24V servo permanent magnet synchronous motors — not AC asynchronous motors. This eliminates the electric leakage risk common to older gate systems and delivers smoother, more precise motion control.

**Step 2 — The Control Board**
The brain of the system. Programmable parameters control open/close speed, auto-close delay, deceleration position, and obstacle response. Our sliding operator features 10 independently adjustable parameters (P-1 through P-10).

**Step 3 — Safety System**
Photocells and safety edges detect obstructions. If anything breaks the beam or contacts the safety edge, the gate stops immediately and reverses — protecting people, vehicles, and the gate itself. Non-negotiable on any professional installation.

**Step 4 — Power & Battery Backup**
All Open Bear operators support battery backup modules. With power outages common across North America, Australia, and Southeast Asia, backup power is a standard specification — not an optional upgrade.

## BLOCK: `[content-buyer-advice·var-a]` — 5 Mistakes When Buying a Gate Opener

**Eyebrow:** BUYER'S ADVICE

**H2:** 5 Mistakes to Avoid When Buying a Gate Opener

**Subtitle:** After shipping 50,000 units a year to homeowners and property managers across North America, Australia, and Southeast Asia — we've seen every buying mistake. Here's what to avoid.

**Mistake 1 — Buying on price alone**
Cheap motors use plastic gears and unshielded electronics. In heat and dust, they fail within 1–2 years. A quality opener costs more upfront but runs reliably for 10–20 years — and never leaves you stranded.

**Mistake 2 — Ignoring gate weight**
Every motor has a rated load. Exceed it and the gearbox strains and the motor burns out within months. Weigh your gate before you buy — the right model handles your gate's actual weight, not an estimate.

**Mistake 3 — Skipping safety sensors**
Obstacle detection and safety edges are not optional extras. A gate that closes on a person, a child, or a pet is a serious risk. All Open Bear openers support safety sensor integration as standard.

**Mistake 4 — Choosing the wrong opener type**
A swing gate opener cannot drive a sliding gate. Getting this wrong means a full replacement at full cost. Match the opener type to your gate before you order.

**Mistake 5 — No battery backup**
A gate with no battery backup locks you out — or traps your car — during a power cut. Battery backup is standard on all Open Bear models. Do not leave it out.

## BLOCK: `[features-cards·var-b]` — Core Technology Advantages

**Eyebrow:** WHY OPEN BEAR

**H2:** The Technology Behind the Motor

**Subtitle:** Every Open Bear operator is built on the same core platform — servo permanent magnet synchronous motor technology at DC24V safety voltage.

**Feature 1 — DC24V Safety Voltage**
Traditional AC gate motors carry a live shock risk. All Open Bear systems run on 24V DC — below the threshold that causes electric shock. No leakage risk. Easier compliance in regulated markets like North America and Europe.

**Feature 2 — Servo Motor Precision**
Servo permanent magnet synchronous motors are faster to respond and more accurate than standard induction motors. Your gate opens at exactly the speed you set — consistent every cycle.

**Feature 3 — Extreme Temperature Range**
Industrial and heavy-duty models operate from -35°C to 70°C. Light swing operators work from -25°C to 70°C. Whether you're supplying Siberia or Singapore, the spec holds.

**Feature 4 — QC on Every Unit**
Before any unit leaves the factory, it is tested on an oscilloscope — waveform verification of the motor signal under load. Not batch testing. Every unit.
